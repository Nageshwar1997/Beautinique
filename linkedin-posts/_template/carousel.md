## Common prompt (har slide se pehle)

Har image ke liye pehle ye block paste karo, phir usi message me neeche slide ka block. Ye saare posts me same rehta hai.

```text
Create exactly ONE image: a single finished slide, not variants, not a grid.
Size: horizontal landscape, aspect ratio 16:9 (wider than tall; not portrait, not square).
Style: flat, clean editorial illustration on a flat soft blush background (#FFF3F5). Brand rose #D6336C is the main accent, with lighter rose, blush and nude tints. Shapes have thin dark outlines. All text in a bold modern geometric sans-serif like Montserrat, dark warm grey #403935 unless stated. Lots of empty space; keep everything at least 80 px from the edges. Text large enough to read on a phone. No real company logos, no photos of people, no text other than the quoted text below.
Layout rule: text on the left half, illustration on the right half, unless the slide block says otherwise. Headline very large (at least one eighth of the image height); smallest text at least one twentieth of the image height.
```
