## Prompt (saari slides ek saath)

```text
Create <N> separate images, one for each slide below, in order (Slide 1 to Slide <N>). Each image is one finished slide: not variants of one slide, not a grid or collage, and no slide numbers or labels on the images.
Size (every image): horizontal landscape, aspect ratio 16:9 (wider than tall; not portrait, not square).
Style (every image): flat, clean editorial illustration on a flat soft blush background (#FFF3F5). Brand rose #D6336C is the main accent, with lighter rose, blush and nude tints. Shapes have thin dark outlines. All text in a bold modern geometric sans-serif like Montserrat, dark warm grey #403935 unless stated. Lots of empty space; keep everything at least 80 px from the edges. Text large enough to read on a phone. No real company logos, no photos of people, no text other than that slide's quoted text.
Layout rule (every image): text on the left half, illustration on the right half, unless that slide's Layout says otherwise. Headline very large (at least one eighth of the image height); smallest text at least one twentieth of the image height.
Keep the same background, colours, outline style and font on all <N> images so they look like one set.

Slide 1
Layout: <left: the text, top to bottom; right: what the picture shows>
Text (spell exactly, use nothing else): "<text 1>" | "<text 2>"

Slide 2
Layout: ...
Text (spell exactly, use nothing else): "..."
```
