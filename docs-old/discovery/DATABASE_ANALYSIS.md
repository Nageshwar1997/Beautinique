## 2. Saajhe niyam

### 2.1 Ids aur document numbers

1. **MongoDB `_id`:** har entity document ka `_id` ObjectId (12 bytes, samay ke kram me). Naam se pehchane jaane wale chhote records (settings, leases, roles, counters, din ki ginti) ka `_id` hi naam (string), taaki alag unique index na lage. Zaroorat: M0 ki jagah (R-031).
2. **Doosri service ka reference:** ObjectId ke roop me hi rakha jaaye (field ke naam me `Id`, jaise `sellerId`); events me 24 hex ka string. Finance (PostgreSQL) me `char(24)` domain `ext_id`, CHECK `^[0-9a-f]{24}$`.
3. **Finance ke apne ids:** `bigint generated always as identity` (chhota, tez). Ye ids bahar nahi jaate; bahar sirf document number (§1.7).
4. **Client ke banaye ids:** Delivery app ke offline kaam aur idempotency keys UUIDv7 (§5 niyam 6); DB me sirf unka hash (SHA-256, 32 bytes BinData), kyunki keys log ya store nahi hoti (§8 niyam 3).
5. **Event id:** `eventId` ObjectId (producer ka outbox `_id`); `processed_events` me (eventId, subscriber).
6. **Id pe bharosa nahi:** har query owner ya scope ke filter ke saath (SR-033, SR-034); ObjectId andaaze layak hai, kabhi secret nahi.
7. **Bahar dikhne wale numbers (tax ke alawa):** har service ka `counters` collection, `$inc` se (gap theek, tax document nahi). Roop DB-5 ke jawab pe; parcel `<orderNo>-P<n>`, return, daawa, ASN, trip aur payout batch ka apna prefix (DBB-1 ki table, report §6).
8. **Tax ke document numbers:** sirf finance me, §1.7 ke niyam se, bina gap (report §3.7).

### 2.2 Paisa, rates aur ginti

1. **Rakam:** integer paise. MongoDB me har rakam BSON `long` (Int64), ek saajhe money codec se likhi jaaye, aur collection validator `bsonType: "long"` maange. Wajah: Node driver JS ka bada number `double` me likhta hai; platform ke mahine ke jod ₹2.14 crore (int32 ki seema) paar kar sakte hain (D-283). PostgreSQL me `bigint`.
2. **Rates:** basis points, `int` (18% = 1800), CHECK 0 se 10000 (§1.1 niyam 2).
3. **Float kabhi nahi:** koi rakam `double` ya `Decimal128` me nahi; JSON me rakam integer (zod `int().nonnegative()` jahan laagu).
4. **Hisaab ka ek raasta:** taxable value, CGST aur SGST §1.8 niyam 1 se; bantwara "largest remainder" se (§1.8 niyam 3, SR-047); return pe asli line ki rakam se anupaat (§1.8 niyam 5). Ye ek saajhe package me, mutation tests ke saath (CLAUDE.md).
5. **Snapshot:** order line pe us pal ka daam, batch ka MRP, HSN, rate, tax aur chhoot ka hissa (kisne diya, D-101) jam jaata hai; baad me catalog badle to order nahi badalta (SR-044).
6. **Quantity:** `int`, CHECK > 0; stock ke counters `int`, kabhi minus nahi (INV-COM-1).

### 2.3 Samay

1. Har timestamp UTC (BSON `date`, PostgreSQL `timestamptz`), server ki ghadi se (§8 niyam 10, CL-106). Client ka samay sirf `deviceAt` (SR-055).
2. **IST din:** jahan din se ginti ya query ho (emails ki chhat D-142, aankde D-149, audit ka din, timers ke roz ke kaam), wahan alag field `istDay` (`YYYY-MM-DD` string), likhte waqt ek helper se. Query me UTC se din nikalna nahi.
3. **Finance:** `doc_date date` (IST tareekh), `period char(7)` (`YYYY-MM`, IST), `fy smallint` (jaise `2627`), teeno document ke IST din se (§1.1 niyam 6). CHECK: `fy` aur `period` `doc_date` se mel khaayein.
4. **Expiry:** batch ka `expiryDate` system ka aakhri din (D-232 ke roop se), IST din; dikhane ke liye mahina aur saal (D-067).

### 2.4 Versions aur concurrency

1. **Aggregate ka `version`:** har badalne wale aggregate (order, parcel, return, listing, batch ka balance, seller, case, payout batch) pe `version` int. Har update `{_id, version: n}` pe, `$inc: {version: 1}`; mel na khaaye to 409 (§5 niyam 9). Event ke envelope ka `aggregate.version` yahi (§3 niyam 5, 7).
2. **Halat ki shart:** state badalne wale update me purani halat filter me (`{status: "picked"}`), taaki do scans ya do staff ek kaam do baar na karein; doosra 409 paata hai.
3. **Unique keys se double ka rasta band:** jahan "ek hi" ho (ek customer ek product ek review D-135; ek parcel ek QR SR-050; ek refund request ek refund; ek return ek pickup) wahan unique ya partial unique index.
4. **Double submit:** §5 niyam 10 ke routes pe idempotency record (report §2.7); baaki pe halat ki shart.
5. **Ginti ki seema atomic:** din ki chhat, staff ki rakam ki seema aur per-customer seemaayein (D-142, D-188, D-204) ek counter document pe conditional `$inc` (`{sent: {$lt: cap}}`), usi transaction me jisme kaam.
6. **Hot document se bachav:** ek document pe bahut likhna sirf jahan zaroori (batch ka balance, din ka counter); audit ki chain jaisi cheez roz ke job me banti hai, har entry pe ek head document nahi (report §3.9).

### 2.5 Transactions

**MongoDB (har service ka ek helper):**

1. `withTransaction(fn)`: snapshot read, majority write; `TransientTransactionError` pe poora kaam dobara, `UnknownTransactionCommitResult` pe sirf commit dobara, chhote backoff aur jitter ke saath; kul misaal 10 second, phir 503 (§5 niyam 9).
2. Jawab commit ke baad hi (§3 niyam 1). Badlav, outbox event, `audit.recorded` event (§3 niyam 10) aur consumer ka `processed_events` ek hi transaction me.
3. Transaction ke andar queries ek ke baad ek (parallel nahi; lint aur runtime ka pehra); query ki galti pakad ke aage nahi badhna; bahar ki koi call (provider, facade, finance) transaction ke andar nahi.
4. Chhote transactions: misaal 100 documents aur 1 second ke andar; har query `maxTimeMS` misaal 2 second (SR-007). Lambe kaam (archive, migration, export) chhote, dobara chalne layak kadmon me, bina transaction ke ya chhote transactions me.
5. Ek transaction sirf ek service ke cluster me; do services ke beech kabhi nahi (D-011). Jahan do jagah likhna ho (pack pe invoice), wahan idempotent kadam (report §3.5, INV-COM-11).

**PostgreSQL (finance):**

6. `READ COMMITTED` default; number ke liye series row `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` (§1.7 niyam 3); ek pack me kai sellers ke invoice hon to series rows (issuer, type) ke kram me lock, taaki deadlock na ho.
7. `40001` aur `40P01` pe poora transaction misaal 3 baar dobara; `statement_timeout` misaal 5 s aur `idle_in_transaction_session_timeout` misaal 10 s (SR-007).
8. Sirf parameterized queries (SR-069); query builder ya repository function, string jodna nahi.

### 2.6 Naam

1. Collection ka naam `<module>_<cheez>` lowercase snake (D-050): jaise `stock_batches`, `order_orders`, `review_reviews`.
2. Har service ki platform collections bina prefix, ARCHITECTURE ke naam se: `outbox`, `processed_events`, `dead_letters`, `timers`, `leases`, `idempotency_keys`, `migrations`, `settings`, `sealed_records`, `daily_stats`, `counters`.
3. Events se bani copy `copy_<cheez>` (jaise `copy_area`), taaki sach aur copy kabhi na milein.
4. Fields camelCase; index ka naam fields se (`accountId_1_placedAt_-1`) ya maqsad se, aur ek naam ke neeche index ka roop kabhi nahi badalta (report §2.13).
5. Finance me har module ka PostgreSQL schema: `ledger`, `doc`, `payout`, `tax`, `recon`, `archive`, `sys`. Tables snake_case.

Niyam:

1. **Dispatcher:** `pendingCount > 0` aur `nextAttemptAt <= now` wale events lease ke peeche uthata hai; ek aggregate ka pehla pending event fail ho to usi subscriber ke liye us aggregate ke agle event nahi uthte (§3 niyam 5).
2. **Subscriber band ho (jaise Neon ke ghante khatam, R-014):** koshish ki ginti nahi badhti, sirf `nextAttemptAt` aage; dead letter sirf handler ki apni galti pe (report §12 point 3).
3. **TTL ka kram:** `processed_events` (14 din) > outbox ka purge (7 din) + sabse lamba retry (misaal 6 ghante). Ye kram test me jaancha jaaye.
4. **Idempotency:** pehli request `state: running` daalti hai (`_id` unique, to doosri ko 409); kaam ke saath usi transaction me jawab `done`; 5xx pe record hat-ta hai. Body ka hash milaan; alag body pe 422. Jawab gzip (100 KB tak, §5 niyam 3). Key kabhi saade roop me nahi.
5. **Finance me:** wahi tables `sys` schema me (PostgreSQL me TTL nahi, isliye roz ke batch me `DELETE ... WHERE purge_at < now()` chhote tukdon me).

### 2.8 Events se bani copies

1. Copy document me `srcVersion` (producer ka `aggregate.version`); upsert sirf jab aane wala version bada ho (`{_id, srcVersion: {$lt: v}}`), taaki ulte kram ke events purani halat na likhein (§3 niyam 5, SR-049 ki soch).
2. Copy sirf wahi fields jo consumer ke kaam me (daam aur listing commerce me, stock ki halat catalog me); personal data ki copy nahi (§3 niyam 8).
3. Har copy ka "dobara bharna" (resync): producer ka ek release step ya Master ka kaam jo saare aggregates ka abhi ka event dobara outbox me daale; consumer version ke niyam se dobara likhe. Copy kabhi producer ka DB nahi padhti (SR-068).
4. Copy me `syncedAt`; roz ka job copies ki ginti aur sabse purana `syncedAt` ops snapshot me likhe (PR-DB-3).

**Soft delete aur erase:**

1. **Business cheez (product, variant, seller, hub):** kabhi hard delete nahi jab tak koi order ya document use dekhta hai; `status: archived` (ARCHITECTURE §7 niyam 6).
2. **Personal data:** erase = field hatana ya document delete, usi transaction me rok ki copy (`sealed_records`) aur event; "soft delete" ka flag personal data ke liye kaafi nahi (D-250 niyam 1).
3. **Rok ki copy:** `payloadEnc` = hate hue fields ka JSON, service ki alag `hold` key se AES-256-GCM (context: service + collection + sourceId); `eraseAt` setting se (abhi 180 din, 13 May 2027 se 365 din, D-250 niyam 2, D-262). Sirf ek break-glass facade se khule (Master, wajah, audit me, D-250 niyam 2); kisi staff screen ya list API me kabhi nahi.
4. **Photos aur video:** file media me apne samay pe hat-ti hai; rok me sirf record (kab, kisne, kis maamle me, hash) media ke `media_assets` me (D-250 niyam 4); 13 May 2027 se file bhi rok ki jagah me, capture se 1 saal (D-262, report §3.10).
5. **Tax archive aur rok ek tareeke se:** dono encrypted, alag key, har pahunch audit me; par jagah alag: tax archive finance ke `archive` schema me (D-225), rok har service ke apne cluster me (D-011: data service ki deewar se bahar nahi).

### 2.12 Validators

1. Har collection banate waqt `$jsonSchema` validator (`validationLevel: strict`, `validationAction: error`) sirf zaroori fields pe: ids ka type, rakam `long` aur minimum 0, `status` ki tay list, `version` int. Baaki fields khule, taaki naya field bina migration aaye (expand).

3. App ke andar zod schema hi pehla pehra; validator doosra (code ki galti DB tak na pahunche).

### 2.13 Migrations aur release steps

**MongoDB:**

1. Boot pe har service ka release step, `leases` ki lease ke peeche: (1) collections aur validators, (2) indexes, (3) data migrations, (4) Atlas Search ki definitions (catalog). Sabka ek fingerprint `migrations` ke paas; fingerprint wahi ho to kuch nahi chalta.
2. **Index:** naam ke neeche roop kabhi nahi badalta; naya roop naya naam (expand); purana index agle release ki migration me drop (contract), jab koi chalta code use na maange.
3. **Expand aur contract:** naya field pehle optional, padhne wala dono roop samjhe; backfill migration; agla release purana roop hataye. Deploy ke waqt do instance saath (§4 niyam 4), isliye kabhi ek hi release me jodna aur hatana nahi.
4. **Data migration:** idempotent, `_id` ke kram me batches (misaal 200 documents), har batch ke baad thoda ruk ke (M0 ke 100 ops/second, R-006); kahan tak hua ye `migrations` me, taaki beech me ruki migration wahin se chale.
5. **Release user:** validator aur TTL badalne ke liye `collMod` chahiye; ye kaam alag DB user se jo sirf release step me judta hai (F-DB-6, §10 Security).

**PostgreSQL (finance):**

6. Numbered SQL files (`V0001__init.sql`), sirf aage; `sys.schema_migrations` me id aur checksum; badli file pe boot rukta hai.
7. `pg_advisory_xact_lock` ke peeche, migration role se (SR-068); app role ke grants har migration ke baad dobara.
8. **Boot pe nahi:** finance ki migration finance ke pehle kaam (batch, pack, Finance app) pe chalti hai, boot pe nahi, kyunki Render ka har jaagna Neon ko jagaata (D-057, R-014).
9. Expand aur contract wahi; badi table pe index `CONCURRENTLY` (transaction ke bahar).

**Constraints aur triggers:**

1. **Balance:** `CONSTRAINT TRIGGER journal_balanced AFTER INSERT ON ledger.journal_lines DEFERRABLE INITIALLY DEFERRED`; commit pe us journal ki lines ka `sum(debit) = sum(credit)` aur kam se kam 2 lines, warna error (§1.1 niyam 1).
2. **Append-only:** app role ko `journals`, `journal_lines`, `documents`, `document_lines`, `cancellations`, `tax_copies`, `tds_entries` pe sirf `INSERT, SELECT`; saath me `BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE` trigger jo har role pe error de (migration role samet), taaki galti se bhi na ho (§1.1 niyam 3, SR-053, CL-131).
3. **Band mahina:** `BEFORE INSERT ON ledger.journals` trigger: `period` open ho aur `doc_date` ka period wahi ho (§1.1 niyam 7).
4. **FY aur period:** CHECK `fy = fy_of(doc_date)` aur `period = to_char(doc_date, 'YYYY-MM')` (immutable function; §1.1 niyam 6).
5. **Credit note ka hawala:** CHECK credit note ke types pe `original_doc_id IS NOT NULL`; lines me `original_line_no`; credit note ki quantity asli line minus pichhle credit notes se zyada nahi (deferred trigger) (§1.7 niyam 7, §1.8 niyam 5).
6. **Document ka jod:** deferred trigger: `taxable + cgst + sgst + igst = total` aur header = lines ka jod (§1.8).
7. **Pack ki idempotency:** partial `UNIQUE (parcel_ref, seller_party_id, doc_type, pack_seq) WHERE parcel_ref IS NOT NULL` (§2.4 niyam 5).
8. **Rakam:** har rakam `bigint` CHECK `>= 0` (jahan sign ka matlab hai, wahan alag debit aur credit), rates CHECK 0 se 10000.

**Finance ke niyam (data):**

1. **PAN aur bank finance me:** seller aur partner ka PAN number aur bank khaata finance me (TDS ke Form 140 aur 131, payout); catalog me sirf KYC ke dastavez (`assetId`) aur GSTIN. Seller app ka bank aur PAN wala form seedha finance ka route (SR-052).
2. **Parties events se:** seller (code, legal naam, GSTINs, state) `catalog.seller.statusChanged` se; partner `logistics.partner.activated` se; customer party sirf id (koi naam nahi).
3. **Invoice ke buyer ka block:** pack ke sync call me commerce state, shehar, pincode deta hai; naam aur pata sirf jab ₹50,000 ya zyada ho ya customer ne maanga (D-226), aur wo seedha `archive.tax_copies` me encrypted (alag `taxArchive` key). Isliye delete pe finance me kuch nahi badalta (D-225 niyam 3).
4. **PDF:** jaari document ke na badalne wale data + `template_version` se, finance ke facade pe (§7 niyam 7); naam aur pata sirf finance role ke liye archive se, `archive.access_log` aur audit me.
5. **Purge:** `sys` ki tables ka purge roz ke batch me chhote tukdon me (PostgreSQL me TTL nahi).
6. **Driver:** `pg` (node-postgres) aur ek type-safe query builder (misaal Kysely); ORM nahi (R-007 ki memory, SR-069). Pool 2, idle timeout chhota (misaal 10 s), taaki Neon so sake (F-DB-2).
7. **Neon ke CU-ghante:** har wake kam se kam misaal 5 minute ka; isliye ek batch ek connection aur ek ke baad ek chhote transactions; triggers aur indexes ka kharcha wake ke saamne chhota. ₹0 pe 100 orders/din: lagbhag 56 batch-khidkiyan/din × 5 minute × 0.25 CU ≈ 35 CU-ghante + pack aur Finance app ≈ 40 se 45 (D-288 ke andaaze jaisa). D-283 target pe 15 minute ki har khidki aur warehouse ke ghanton me lagataar pack: lagbhag 60 + 75 > 100 CU-ghante (report §4.5).
8. **Finance ka dispatcher aur ticker:** Neon tabhi padha jaaye jab finance pehle se jaaga ho (batch, pack, Finance app) aur roz ke ek batch me; har 5 second ka poll aur har minute ka ticker finance pe nahi (report §12 point 2).

### 4.2 Kaun pehle bharta hai

1. **Design target pe:** audit (30 din ki khidki pe ~1 mahina), phir commerce (~7 hafte) aur finance (~50 din). Matlab: D-283 ka target ₹0 pe kuch hafte bhi nahi chalta; ye sirf code aur tests ka target hai (D-283), aur load test laptop pe (§9 niyam 6).
2. **₹0 ki asli seema pe:** finance pehle (~1.4 saal agar Neon 0.5 GB), phir commerce (~1.7 saal agar DB-3 ka archive na ho). Audit 2 saal me ~45% (R-031 ka ~250 orders/din wala hisaab theek).
3. Isliye do jagah faisla chahiye: DB-3 (commerce) aur DB-6 (finance). Baaki clusters TTL aur chhote archive se chalte hain.

### 4.3 Bytes kaise ghatein (sab clusters)

1. Chhote field naam nahi (padhne me mushkil); bachat sirf bade hisson se: lambe text gzip, purane records ke chunks, TTL.
2. Ek document me embedded lines (order) ek alag collection se sasta (har document ka `_id` index bachta).
3. Index sirf report §3 ki tables wale; har naya index PR me jagah ke andaaze ke saath.
4. Har collection ka roz ka `collStats` (size + indexes) ops snapshot me; 70% aur 85% pe alert (§9 niyam 5).

### 4.4 Archive aur compression

1. **Mahine ka chunk:** band records (order DB-3, payments, logistics ke parcels, stock movements) har mahine ke `*_archive_chunks` me: JSON lines gzip, `sha256`, 16 MB ki document seema se pehle naya `part`.
2. **Chhota saar:** archive hue order ke liye `order_index` (0.2 KB), taaki list aur number se dhoondh chale.
3. **Kram:** chunk likho → `sha256` jaancho → asli records delete, chhote batches me (M0 ke ops, R-006). Har kadam dobara chalne layak.
4. **Restore:** chunk se ek record kholna ek facade function; poora mahina laptop pe restore (backup command jaisa).
5. **Audit:** §8 niyam 4 jaisa, din ka chunk (report §3.9).

### DBB-2. Transactions, concurrency aur platform collections

1. **DBB-2.1** Har MongoDB service ka ek transaction helper: snapshot read, majority write, transient error pe poora kaam dobara, commit ka anjaana nateeja ho to sirf commit dobara; kul misaal 10 second, phir 503.
2. **DBB-2.2** Badlav, outbox event, `audit.recorded` event aur (consumer me) `processed_events` ek transaction me; jawab commit ke baad.
3. **DBB-2.3** Transaction ke andar queries ek ke baad ek; bahar ki koi call nahi; misaal 100 documents aur 1 second ke andar; har query `maxTimeMS` misaal 2 second.
4. **DBB-2.4** Transaction ek hi service ke cluster me; do jagah likhna ho to idempotent kadam aur halat ki shart.
5. **DBB-2.5** Har badalne wale aggregate pe `version`; update `{_id, version}` pe, mel na ho to 409; event ka `aggregate.version` yahi.
6. **DBB-2.6** Halat badalne wale update me purani halat filter me, taaki do scans ya do staff ek kaam do baar na karein.
7. **DBB-2.7** "Ek hi" wali cheezein (ek customer ek product ek review, ek parcel ek khula QR, ek refund request ek refund) unique ya partial unique index se.
8. **DBB-2.8** Din ki chhat, staff ki rakam ki seema aur per-customer seemaayein ek counter document pe conditional `$inc` se, kaam ke transaction me.
9. **DBB-2.9** Event se bani copy (`copy_<cheez>`) me `srcVersion`; upsert sirf bade version pe; har copy ka resync producer ke "abhi ka event dobara" se.

## 12. Tay faislon pe dhyaan

Ye faisle dobara nahi khol rahe; sirf data ki dikkat, wajah aur sabse chhota sudhaar.

1. **ARCHITECTURE §2.3 aur §3 niyam 8: logistics ko address ka raasta nahi.** Partner ke offline data (SR-023) aur label ke liye naam, address aur phone chahiye, par events me poora address mana hai aur sync list me logistics se commerce ki call nahi. **Sudhaar:** §2.3 me ek row: "logistics → commerce: assign aur Delivery sync ke waqt parcel ka naam, address aur phone (resolver); logistics me copy nahi".
2. **§3 niyam 3 aur §4 niyam 1 finance pe:** har 5 second ka outbox poll aur har minute ka ticker Neon ko jagaata rahega (D-057, D-288, R-014). **Sudhaar:** finance ka dispatcher aur ticker sirf jab finance pehle se jaaga ho (batch, pack, Finance app) aur roz ke ek batch me (DBB-6.13).
3. **§3 niyam 6 aur R-014:** Neon ke ghante mahine ke ant tak khatam hon to finance ke saare events 6 ghante me dead letter me. **Sudhaar:** subscriber band ho to koshish ki ginti nahi badhti, sirf `nextAttemptAt` aage (DBB-2.11).
4. **§2.2 finance ke "Leta hai" me delivered ka event nahi:** return window ke baad payable (D-081) aur delivery pe UPI wale order ka delivery fee invoice (§1.6) delivered ke pal pe tike hain. **Sudhaar:** finance `commerce.orderLine.delivered` bhi le.
5. **§1.7 niyam 1 ki misaal `S01`:** 2 ank 99 sellers tak; D-283 me 500. **Sudhaar:** seller ka code 3 characters (DBB-1.10); 16 ki seema me fit.
6. **D-225 niyam 2 aur D-226 niyam 2 (aur SR-088):** ₹50,000 ya zyada ke invoice ki seller ki copy me naam aur pata hai, par delete ke baad naam aur pata sirf finance ke archive me. Seller ko apne records 72 mahine rakhne hain (CL-021). **Sudhaar:** delete ke baad bhi seller ki ek-ek download archive ki copy se, finance ke raaste, audit me; ya copy jaari hote hi. Lawyer (ya CA) se nahi jaancha (CL-020, CL-021).
7. **D-250 niyam 2 ("13 May 2027 se sab data 1 saal") aur chhote TTL:** OTP ke hash, idempotency ke jawab, sessions, outbox aur inbox jaise records ki har deletion pe rok ki copy bahut bhaari (har cluster ki jagah). **Sudhaar:** har collection ki retention class (report §2.9); `tech` class ke records (jinka personal data request logs me pehle se hai) rok me nahi. Iski seema Lawyer se nahi jaanchi (CL-093).
8. **D-118 niyam 2 aur D-064 niyam 3:** der se aaye payment pe "stock ho to order", par tab tak dikhaya batch bik chuka ho sakta hai aur agle batch ka MRP ya expiry alag. **Sudhaar:** dobara reservation sirf usi MRP aur kam se kam utni expiry wale batch se; warna D-118 ka refund (INV-COM-6).
9. **SR-063 ("baaki codes sirf hash") aur §2.2 logistics ("delivery codes (encrypted)"):** delivery code customer ke app aur email me dikhna hai (D-072), to hash se kaam nahi chalta. **Sudhaar:** SR-063 me ek line: "delivery code encrypted, kyunki dikhana zaroori; milaan server pe; baaki codes sirf hash".
10. **§1.7 niyam 4 aur §2.4 niyam 5:** pack pe invoice jaari hua par pack poora nahi hua, ya dobara pack hua (pick pe kami, D-090), to dispatch se pehle invoice cancel ka raasta finance tak nahi hai (finance ke events me pack ka chhootna nahi; sync list me cancel nahi). **Sudhaar:** commerce ka event `commerce.parcel.packVoided` (parcel, seller, `packSeq`), finance use cancel kare; naya pack naye `packSeq` se.
11. **§8 niyam 4 aur D-283:** 30 din ki bina compression audit ki khidki target pe akele ~360 MB (cluster ka 70%). **Sudhaar:** koi doc badlav nahi (number "misaal" hai); chunk ki umar setting volume ke saath (DBB-8.3).
12. **§9 ki ₹0 table me Neon ki jagah nahi:** sirf CU-ghante likhe; books 8 FY (§1.1 niyam 11) ₹0 pe nahi samaate. **Sudhaar:** table me "Neon jagah: 0.5 GB ya 1 GB (jaanch); finance ₹0 pe 100 orders/din pe 1.4 se 2.8 saal" ki line, aur DB-6.
