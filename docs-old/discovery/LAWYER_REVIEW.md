## 1. Part 1: Platform aur seller ka kanooni dhaancha

### 1.1 Marketplace ya inventory model, aur FDI

- **Sawal:** brands ka maal hamare warehouse me (D-060), aur fulfilment aur delivery apni (D-014). Kya hum marketplace hain? Kabhi foreign investment aaye to FDI policy kya kehti hai (fulfilment, apna brand, ek vendor pe nirbharta)?
- **Raay:**
  1. **Hum marketplace hain.** Kasauti maal ka maalikana hai, warehouse kiska hai ye nahi. E-Commerce Rules ki paribhasha me marketplace wo jo buyers aur sellers ke beech platform deta hai, aur inventory entity wo jo maal ki maalik ho aur seedha bechti ho [E1, rule 3(1)]. D-060 (maalik bikne tak brand), D-030 (invoice seller ke GSTIN se), D-061 (daam seller ka) aur D-062 (expiry ka nuksaan brand ka) is kasauti pe poore utarte hain. Apna warehouse, packing aur delivery "support services" hain, maalikana nahi.
  2. **Inventory jaisa dikhne ke khatre** (abhi koi nahi, par design me bachna): platform ka maal khareedna ya bechna; maal ka jokhim platform pe aana (expiry, na bikne ka nuksaan); platform ka daam tay karna; platform ka seller ke liye maal mangwana; aur "Sold by" me platform ka naam.
  3. **Sirf Indian-owned company (abhi, D-040):** FDI policy ki e-commerce shartein laagu hi nahi. Policy me "e-commerce entity" wahi company hai jo "owned or controlled by a person resident outside India" ho [A1, 5.2.15.2.2(ii)]. To aaj apna brand, platform ke diye discount (R2, D-101) aur fulfilment, sab FDI ki nazar se khule hain. Baaki kanoon (E-Commerce Rules, CPA, competition) phir bhi laagu.
  4. **Foreign investment aaye to (D-040 khule tab):** marketplace me 100% automatic route; inventory model me FDI mana [A1, 5.2.15.2.3]. Shartein [A1, 5.2.15.2.4]:
     - (iii) warehousing, logistics, order fulfilment, call centre aur payment collection jaisi support services di ja sakti hain: **hamara fulfilment model chal jaata hai**.
     - (iv) inventory pe maalikana ya control nahi; kisi vendor ki 25% se zyada khareed platform ya uski group companies se ho to inventory "controlled" maani jaati hai.
     - (v) jis company me platform ya uski group companies ki equity ho, ya jiski inventory pe unka control ho, wo platform pe nahi bech sakti: **Unibelle ke liye seedha khatra**. Agar Beautinique Pvt Ltd ya uski group company ki Unibelle Cosmetics Pvt Ltd me koi bhi equity hai, to FDI ke baad Unibelle platform pe nahi bik sakta.
     - (vi), (viii): seller ki jaankari page pe; delivery ke baad customer ki santushti aur warranty seller ki zimmedari.
     - (vii): payment ki suvidha RBI ke niyam ke hisaab se.
     - (ix): platform daam pe "directly or indirectly" asar nahi daalta; services sab vendors ko arm's length pe aur bina bhed; group companies ka cashback bhi bina bhed.
     - (xi) kisi seller ko sirf hamare platform pe bechne ko majboor nahi; (xii) har saal 30 September tak statutory auditor ki report.
  5. **"Group Company"** = do enterprises jinme ek doosri me 26% ya zyada voting chala sake, ya board ke 50% se zyada log rakh sake [A1, definitions]. Agar owner khud dono companies ka shareholder hai aur dono companies ek doosre me shares nahi rakhti, to Unibelle "group company" hai ya nahi, padhai pe tika hai (Anishchit). Bade platforms ne 2019 ke baad aise sellers me apni equity hata di thi (aam jaankari, is run me jaancha nahi).
  6. **Vendor concentration:** 2018 ke baad policy me "ek vendor platform ki 25% se zyada bikri na kare" wali seema nahi dikhti; ab 25% ki kasauti vendor ki **khareed** pe hai (upar (iv)). Unibelle platform se maal nahi khareedta, to ye kasauti abhi nahi lagti.
  7. 2026 me Press Note 3 se export ke liye inventory model khula [A2, secondary]; ghar ke customers pe iska asar nahi.
- **Kanoon:** E-Commerce Rules 2020 rule 3(1) [E1]; Consolidated FDI Policy 2020 para 5.2.15.2 aur Group Company [A1], primary document, secondary host, 2026-11-20; Press Note 3 (2026) [A2], secondary.
- **Hamare faislon se mel:** D-030, D-060, D-061, D-062, D-139 (kisi seller ka boost nahi, apna brand bhi) aur D-031 (apna brand baaki sellers jaisa, wahi fees) marketplace ke saath mel khaate hain. D-040 (FDI nahi) ki wajah se aaj FDI ka takrav nahi. D-101 (platform ka discount, R2) FDI ke baad "daam pe asar" ki shart (ix) se takra sakta hai.
- **Prastaav:** (1) COMPLIANCE §13 "Nazar" me ek row: "Foreign investment (D-040) khule to FDI ki e-commerce shartein: Unibelle ki equity, arm's length fees, platform ke discount, auditor ki report". (2) Abhi se seller record me "related party" ke saath "platform ya group ki equity %" ka field (dummy), taaki baad me kasauti ek query ho. (3) Terms aur seller agreement me saaf likhna: maal seller ka, platform sirf facilitator aur fulfilment service deta hai.
- **Bharosa:** marketplace hona: Pakka. FDI shartein: Pakka (text padha). Unibelle ka "group company" hona: Anishchit.
- **Owner ka faisla chahiye?** Abhi nahi (D-040 tay). Nazar wali row ki haan chahiye.

### 1.2 IT Act s.79 ki chhoot aur E-Commerce Rules ki marketplace shartein

- **Sawal:** s.79 ki chhoot rakhne ke liye kya karna hai, aur apna warehouse, delivery aur apna brand use kahan kamzor karte hain?
- **Raay:**
  1. **Chhoot ka daayra:** s.79 sirf **teesre ki jaankari** (seller ki listing, reviews, sawal-jawab, Creator ka content) ki zimmedari se bachata hai [A3, s.79(1) aur Explanation]. Apne kaam (warehouse me rakhna, pack karna, delivery, refund, apna likha content jaise "Bestseller", kram, routines) pe s.79 kabhi laagu nahi hota; un pe hum seedhe zimmedar hain (CPA, contract, §1.5).
  2. **Shartein:** (a) platform transmission shuru na kare, receiver na chune, aur jaankari na chune ya badle (s.79(2)(b)); (b) due diligence aur IT Rules maane (s.79(2)(c)); (c) galat kaam me saath na de, aur sarkari suchna ya "actual knowledge" pe jaldi hataaye (s.79(3)) [A3]. E-Commerce Rules rule 5(1) yahi shart marketplace pe lagata hai, aur rule 5(2) se 5(5) aur rule 4 ki duties bhi [E1].
  3. **Warehouse aur delivery:** 2018 ke Louboutin faisle me "active" platform ki chhoot jaane ki baat thi; 2020 me Division Bench (Amazon v. Amway) ne prima facie kaha ki marketplace ki value-added services (logistics, packaging) se chhoot apne aap nahi jaati [A4, secondary]. Supreme Court ne ye sawal tay nahi kiya. Meri padhai: fulfilment se listing ke content ki chhoot nahi jaati, jab tak hum content chunte ya badalte nahi. Par fulfilment me hamari laparwahi ka nuksaan hamara hi hai.
  4. **Apna brand:** Unibelle ki listing ka content agar platform ke staff likhte ya badalte hain, to us content pe platform "author" jaisa dikhta hai aur s.79(2)(b) toot-ta hai. 2025 me Delhi HC ne ek bade platform ke group ke apne brand pe trademark ki galti ke liye group ki company ko seedha zimmedar mana, aur "mere intermediary" ki daleel nahi chali [A5, secondary]. Seekh: apne brand pe safe harbour ki ummeed na rakhein; Unibelle ko poora manufacturer aur seller maan ke chalein.
  5. **Moderation se chhoot nahi jaati:** IT Rules me 3(1)(b) ki shreniyon ka content apni marzi se ya shikayat pe hatana s.79(2) ki shart nahi todta (rule 3(1)(d) ka proviso, [U1]; is run me dobara nahi khola).
- **Kanoon:** IT Act s.79 [A3], secondary host; E-Commerce Rules rules 4, 5 [E1]; IT Rules 2021 rule 3, 3A [U1], [U2]; faisle [A4], [A5] secondary; sab 2026-11-20.
- **Hamare faislon se mel:** D-098 niyam 3 (staff seller ka field khud nahi badalta) s.79 ki sabse zaroori shart hai: mel. D-136, D-146, D-251 (moderation aur samay), D-163 (grievance), CL-085 (baar-baar ullanghan), D-240 (seller ka undertaking) mel. D-031 (Unibelle alag seller account, koi shortcut nahi) theek; jokhim tab jab Own-Brand Manager platform staff ke haq se Unibelle ka content badle.
- **Prastaav:** (1) Unibelle ka content sirf Unibelle ke seller account se (Own-Brand Manager wahan seller user, platform staff ke haq ke bina), aur Unibelle ko utna hi data jitna kisi bhi seller ko; doosre sellers ka data kabhi nahi (rule 5(4) aur D-139 ke saath). (2) Platform ka apna content (badges, kram, routines, Expert ki salah) system me "platform content" ke tag se, taaki pata rahe kaunsa content s.79 me nahi. (3) Terms me 3(1)(b) ki list, IP ki shikayat ka raasta, aur "hum content ke author nahi" ki line (D-255). (4) Listing ka staff wala sudhaar kabhi seedha nahi; seller ko wajah ke saath wapas (D-098 jaisa).
- **Bharosa:** shartein: Pakka (text). Fulfilment se chhoot na jaana: Sambhav (High Court ka prima facie raay, vivaad baaki). Apne brand pe chhoot na milna: Sambhav.
- **Owner ka faisla chahiye?** Prastaav (1) ki haan (§4 #8).

### 1.3 Sellers ka paisa: payment aggregator, settlement aur kaat

- **Sawal:** customer ka saara paisa platform ke gateway account me (D-093), phir hafte ka payout (D-081), pehle fees aur udhaar ki kaat (D-071). Kya ye RBI ke niyam me theek hai?
- **Raay:**
  1. **Hum payment aggregator nahi, PA ke merchant hain.** PA Master Direction me PA wo jo merchants ke liye customers ka paisa jama karke merchants ko settle kare (para 4(i)); "merchant" me marketplace bhi shaamil (4(f)); PA khud marketplace ka kaam nahi kar sakta (10(b)); aur PA dekhe ki uska marketplace merchant kisi aise seller ke liye paisa na le jo marketplace pe onboarded nahi (13(f)) [C4]. Aakhri niyam maan ke chalta hai ki **marketplace apne sellers ke liye paisa leta hai**. To text ki banawat ye kehti hai ki ek marketplace PA ke zariye apne sellers ka paisa le sakta hai, bina khud PA authorisation ke (para 5(b) sirf "operating as a PA" pe).
  2. **Nodal account ka purana niyam:** 2009 ke "intermediaries" wale directions (nodal account aur T+3 settlement) para 20 se hat gaye (sirf kuch puraane PA ke liye bache) [C4]. To marketplace pe RBI ka koi alag escrow ya nodal ka niyam nahi mila. Escrow ka niyam PA pe hai (16(a), Table 1).
  3. **Seller ko kab dena hai:** RBI ka samay PA se merchant (yani hum) tak hai: "agreement ke hisaab se", jo fair ho aur samay saaf likhe (Table 1). Hum se seller tak ka samay **contract ki baat hai**; E-Commerce Rules me bhi koi samay nahi [E1]. D-081 (return window ke baad payable, hafte me payout) theek hai, jab tak seller agreement me saaf likha ho.
  4. **Kaat aur set-off:** fees, udhaar (D-071), bharpai (D-063) ka jod-ghata contract ka set-off hai; koi khaas kanoon nahi mila (CL-126). Shart: agreement me haq, har kaat ka statement, aur jhagde ka raasta. TCS aur TDS kanoon se kat-te hain (CA, §6).
  5. **Bhoomika saaf likhna zaroori:** seller agreement aur Terms me platform seller ka **"limited payment collection agent"**: customer ka platform ko diya paisa seller ko diya maana jaaye. Isse customer ka haq saaf, aur sellers ka paisa platform ki kamaai nahi (D-256 ki liability).
  6. **Gateway ki taraf se:** 13(f) ki wajah se gateway marketplace se sellers ki list ya KYC maang sakta hai, aur kai gateways marketplace ke liye alag product (linked accounts) chahte hain. Ye tathya gateway ke terms se, asli business se pehle (D-093 ka baad ka raasta).
  7. **Paisa doobne ka jokhim:** platform ke account me pada sellers ka paisa kanoon me alag "trust" nahi; platform diwaliya ho to seller aam lenadaar ban sakta hai (Sambhav; insolvency kanoon nahi khola). Ledger me liability (D-256) accounting ka hal hai, kanooni suraksha nahi.
- **Kanoon:** RBI PA Master Direction (15 Sep 2025, updated 11 Nov 2026) para 4(e), 4(f), 4(i), 5(b), 10(b), 10(f), 13(f), 16(a), 20, Table 1 [C4], primary, 2026-11-20; E-Commerce Rules [E1]; FDI 5.2.15.2.4(vii) [A1] (sirf FDI pe).
- **Hamare faislon se mel:** D-093, D-081, D-071, D-103, D-256 niyam 4, D-111 (refund usi raaste, 10(f) se mel) sab mel khaate hain. D-034 (cash nahi) se COD ka escrow sawal hi nahi.
- **Prastaav:** (1) Seller agreement me "limited payment collection agent", payout ke din, kam se kam rakam, statement aur set-off (§3.3). (2) Gateway ko maangne pe dene ke liye sellers ki list ka export (naam, GSTIN, onboarding ki tareekh), dummy. (3) Asli business pe sellers ke paise ke liye alag bank account ka sawal (§5 Q5). (4) Payout statement me har kaat ki line aur 30 din (misaal) me aapatti ka raasta (D-164).
- **Bharosa:** marketplace = merchant, PA authorisation nahi: Sambhav (text ki banawat saaf, RBI ne seedha nahi likha). Settlement ka samay contract ka: Pakka (Table 1). Set-off: Sambhav.
- **Owner ka faisla chahiye?** §5 Q5 (alag bank account).

### 1.4 Gift card ka issuer

- **Sawal:** gift card (D-179, D-249) kaun issue kare, aur platform ki bhoomika kya ho sakti hai?
- **Raay:**
  1. **Platform khud gift card issue nahi kar sakta (bina RBI ijazat ke).** Closed system PPI wahi jo issuer **apna** maal ya seva khareedne ke liye de; ye RBI ke niyam se bahar [A6, ¶2.1]. Hamare platform pe maal sellers ka hai (D-060), to platform ka gift card "teesre" ke maal ke liye payment hai: closed system nahi. Baaki PPI sirf RBI ki manzoori wale bank (¶3.1) ya authorised non-bank (¶4.2) de sakte hain. D-249 sahi hai.
  2. **Platform ki do sambhav bhoomikayein:** (a) **merchant:** authorised issuer ka gift card checkout pe payment ke ek tareeke ki tarah (D-249 niyam 1); (b) **co-branding partner:** card pe "Beautinique" ka naam, issuer ke saath; partner India me incorporated company ho, issuer partner ke har kaam ka zimmedar, aur partner ka kaam marketing aur distribution tak [A6, ¶7.11]. Dono me paisa issuer ke escrow me (¶12.3), platform ke paas nahi.
  3. **Gift PPI ke niyam (issuer pe, par hamara design inke andar):** ek card ₹10,000 tak, reload nahi, cash-out nahi (sirf holder ki marzi se source account me wapas), khareedne wale ki KYC (Indian bank ya card se payment pe alag KYC nahi), holder ke kehne pe revalidate (¶10.1); validity aakhri loading se kam se kam 1 saal (¶13.1); expiry se pehle 45 din me holder ko yaad (¶13.2); expiry ke baad bhi non-bank issuer 3 saal tak balance apne munafe me nahi le sakta (¶13.3).
  4. **Unibelle ka apna gift card** (sirf Unibelle ka maal) closed system ho sakta hai, RBI ke bahar; par usse platform pe doosre sellers ka maal nahi khareeda ja sakta. Ye vikalp sirf jaankari ke liye.
- **Kanoon:** RBI Master Directions on PPIs ¶2.1, 3.1, 4.2, 7.11, 10.1, 12.3, 13.1 se 13.3 [A6], primary, 2026-11-20; FAQ [R2]. GST CA ke paas (CL-022).

(2) Co-branding ya sirf merchant, ye gift card banne ke waqt (R2 ke baad) issuer ki shart se tay; abhi design dono sambhale. (3) Gift card ka refund (D-179) issuer ke "source account" niyam ke andar.

- **Owner ka faisla chahiye?** Nahi; D-249 ke andar.

### 1.5 Product liability: brand, platform, apna brand aur delivery

- **Sawal:** CPA 2019 Chapter VI me product manufacturer, product service provider aur product seller ki zimmedari kaise bante?
- **Raay:**
  1. **Paribhashayein:** product manufacturer me wo bhi jo kisi aur ke banaye maal pe "apna mark" lagaye (s.2(36)(iii)); product seller me bechne, distribute, package, label, maintain karne wala ya maal bazaar tak pahunchane me shaamil, aur "electronic service provider" (online marketplace) is se bahar nahi (s.2(37)); product service provider = maal ke baare me koi seva dene wala (s.2(38)) [A7]. "Harm" me maal khud ka nuksaan aur commercial loss nahi; chot, bimari, doosri property ka nuksaan aur unse juda mental dukh shaamil (s.2(22)).
  2. **Brand (manufacturer ya brand owner seller):** s.84: manufacturing defect, design defect, specification se farak, express warranty se mel nahi, ya chetavni aur istemaal ki hidayat ki kami; bina laparwahi ke bhi zimmedar (s.84(2)).
  3. **Authorised distributor seller:** product seller (s.86); asli manufacturer (label wala, CL-029) s.84 me.
  4. **Unibelle Cosmetics Pvt Ltd:** maal kisi aur se banwaye aur apna mark lagaye to bhi **product manufacturer** (s.2(36)(iii)), poori s.84 zimmedari. Iske liye Cosmetics Rules ka licence ya loan licence (dummy) Unibelle ke naam.
  5. **Platform (Beautinique Pvt Ltd):** hum maal rakhte, maintain karte, receiving aur returns pe jaanchte aur pack karte hain, to hum **product seller** hain [A7, s.2(37)]. s.86 me zimmedari sirf in haalaton me: (a) design, packaging ya labelling pe "substantial control"; (b) maal badla; (c) apni alag warranty di; (d) manufacturer ka pata nahi, ya wo India ke kanoon ki pahunch me nahi; (e) jodne, jaanchne ya maintain karne me reasonable care nahi, ya manufacturer ki chetavni aage nahi di, aur wahi nuksaan ki seedhi wajah. Hamare liye asli jokhim (d) (bahar ka brand bina Indian importer) aur (e) (garmi me rakha sunscreen, expired dispatch, chetavni product page pe nahi). Fulfilment ki seva ke liye hum **product service provider** bhi (s.85): seva me kami, laparwahi, ya chetavni na dena.
  6. **Delivery partner aur transporter:** parcel kho jaana ya toot jaana "harm" nahi (maal ka nuksaan), to product liability nahi; ye customer ke saath **seva me kami** (s.2(11)) ka maamla hai, aur delivery fee platform leta hai (CL-012), to customer ke saamne platform zimmedar. Partner aur transporter se vasooli contract se (D-068). Brand ko bharpai D-063 se: hum bailee hain aur D-063 ek "special contract" hai jo kanoon ki aam zimmedari (laparwahi pe) se zyada zimmedari leta hai (Indian Contract Act s.151, 152; is run me text nahi khola, Sambhav).
  7. **Bachav:** maal ka galat istemaal, badlav ya modification ho to product seller pe action nahi (s.87(1)); saaf ya aam jaane-maane khatre pe manufacturer ki chetavni ki zimmedari nahi (s.87(3)).
  8. 2026 ke E-Commerce amendment me marketplace pe koi "fall-back" zimmedari hai ya nahi, poora text na khulne se nahi jaancha [E3].
- **Kanoon:** CPA 2019 s.2(11), 2(17), 2(22), 2(36), 2(37), 2(38), s.84 se 87 [A7], primary text, secondary host, 2026-11-20; E-Commerce Rules rule 5 [E1]; Cosmetics Rules [K1].
- **Hamare faislon se mel:** D-062, D-082, D-124 (pehle expire wala pehle), D-112, D-113 (returns QC), D-127 (importer zaroori: s.86(d) ka jokhim ghatata hai), D-129 (recall), D-130 (skin reaction), D-230 (barcode label likhawat pe nahi: s.86(b) se bachav), CL-068, CL-056 mel. D-063 aur D-068 contract ke taur pe theek.
- **Prastaav:** (1) Terms me saaf: seller ki warranty, platform ki apni koi warranty nahi (s.86(c) se bachav); par customer ke kanooni haq kabhi kam nahi. (2) Seller agreement me product liability, recall aur regulator ke kharche ka indemnity seller ka; platform ki indemnity sirf apni laparwahi aur custody ke nuksaan (D-063) tak. (3) Label pe storage shart wale products (sunscreen, perfume) ke liye warehouse me alag niyam ka record (CL-068 ka hissa). (4) D-130 ki goodwill refund Terms me "bina zimmedari maane" (§3.1).

### 1.6 Gig delivery partners

- **Sawal:** partner contractor hain (D-033); nuksaan ki vasooli (D-068); kya cheez unhe employee banne se bachati hai?
- **Raay:**
  1. **Kanoon ki banawat:** Code on Social Security me "gig worker" wo jo paramparik employer-employee rishte ke **bahar** kaam karke kamaaye (s.2(35)); hum "aggregator" ki do shreniyon me (CL-112) [G1]. Code gig workers ko alag shreni maanta hai, par koi court ye tay kar sakta hai ki asal me rishta employment ka hai. India me kasauti tathyon pe tiki hai (control, kaam ka tareeka, kaun auzaar deta hai, kitni azaadi); koi tay seema nahi (Anishchit). App-based workers ke haq ka ek maamla Supreme Court me chal raha hai (aam jaankari, is run me nahi jaancha).
  2. **Contractor bane rehne ke ishare (design me rakhein):** partner kaam ki offer maan ya chhod sake, aur chhodne pe saza nahi (sirf baar-baar "accept karke chhodna" pe); apni availability khud chune, koi fixed shift ya attendance nahi; doosre platforms pe kaam ki rok nahi; apni gaadi aur kharcha (D-033); kamaai har kaam pe (D-104), salary ya chhutti nahi; koi employee jaisi disciplinary process nahi, sirf contract ki shartein aur account rokne ka saaf kaaran aur appeal; documents aur system me "partner", "staff" nahi.
  3. **Nuksaan ki kaat (D-068, D-103):** contractor pe Code on Wages ki kaat ki seema seedhe nahi lagti, par agar kabhi rishta employment maana gaya to: goods ke nuksaan pe kaat sirf laparwahi ya galti ka nuksaan jitni (s.18(2)(c), s.21(1)), aur **show cause ka mauka diye bina nahi** (s.21(2)), aur register me record (s.21(3)) [A8]. D-068 (saboot, sunwai, staff ka faisla, record) aur D-103 (₹500 ek maamla, mahine me ₹1,500 ya 10%) is kasauti pe bhi tikte hain: mel.
  4. **Apni gaadi:** private two-wheeler se commercial delivery pe registration, permit aur insurance ki halat saaf nahi; 2025 ke aggregator guidelines private bike ko sirf passenger ride ke liye (state ki marzi se) kholte dikhte hain, delivery ke liye nahi; insurance company private policy pe delivery ka claim mana kar sakti hai [A9, secondary]. Anishchit. Partner agreement me valid licence, RC aur aisa insurance jo delivery ka istemaal cover kare, partner ki zimmedari; onboarding pe saboot aur expiry ki yaad.
  5. **State kanoon:** Maharashtra ka 2026 ka gig bill pass hua, iska saboot nahi mila [A10]; Karnataka, Rajasthan jaise states me kaam shuru karne se pehle unke kanoon (CL-112 ki "har state ki row").
- **Kanoon:** Code on Social Security 2020 s.2(2), 2(35), 113, 114 [G1]; Rules 2026 [G3] secondary; Code on Wages s.18(2)(c), 21 [A8] secondary host; [A9], [A10] secondary; sab 2026-11-20.

### 1.7 Online consultation ke experts

- **Sawal:** Skin & Hair Expert aur Beauty Advisor (D-025, CL-121) ki zimmedari kya, aur unki bhoomika aur qualification kya ho?
- **Raay:**
  1. **D-252 ke baad ye medical seva nahi**, to Telemedicine guidelines (sirf RMP ke liye) laagu nahi [H2]. Shart ye ki expert sach me nidaan, dawa ya ilaaj na bataye, aur khud ko "doctor" ya "dermatologist" na kahe. Bina registration medicine practice karna NMC Act s.34 me saza wala kaam hai [A11, secondary]; isliye D-252 ka "ilaaj pe doctor se milein" zaroori hai.
  2. **Zimmedari:** expert hamara staff role hai, to uski galat salah ki zimmedari platform ki (employer ki vicarious liability, aam siddhant). CPA me muft seva "service" nahi (s.2(42)) [A7], to muft consultation (D-172) pe consumer commission me "deficiency" ka case kamzor; par laparwahi ka civil daawa phir bhi ho sakta hai, aur seva kabhi paid hui to CPA poora laagu.
  3. **Qualification:** beauty salah ke liye koi kanooni qualification nahi mili. Par "Expert" kehna ek daawa hai; jhootha ya bina aadhaar ka ho to gumraah karne wala (CPA s.2(28), CL-075). Isliye profile pe sach, jaanchne layak qualification (jaise beauty ya cosmetology ka certificate) aur "medical consultation nahi" ki line.
  4. **Products ki salah:** expert ya advisor jab product sujhaye, wo platform ka apna content hai (s.79 nahi). (a) DMR Act ke mana daawe nahi (CL-123); (b) Unibelle ya kisi related seller ka product sujhaye aur rishta na bataye to endorsement me "material connection" na batana [E5, secondary]; (c) D-139 jaisa: kisi brand ki taraf jhukav nahi.
  5. **Data:** notes sehat ka data (D-207, D-208); Google Meet ka istemaal ho to Meet chalane wala processor list me (D-255 niyam 8), aur call record na ho.
- **Kanoon:** Telemedicine guidelines [H2] (saar); NMC Act s.34 [A11] secondary; CPA s.2(28), 2(42) [A7]; endorsement guidelines [E5] secondary; DMR Act [H1]; sab 2026-11-20.
- **Hamare faislon se mel:** D-025, D-252, D-172, D-207, D-208 mel. Kami: qualification ki shart, aur related brand ki salah pe rishta batana (§4 #4).
- **Prastaav:** (1) Expert aur Advisor ke profile pe qualification ka field aur staff jaanch (dummy). (2) Salah me Unibelle (ya koi related party) ka product ho to uske saath ek tay label "related brand". (3) Booking ke Terms me: "beauty salah, medical nahi; product ka chunaav aapka".
- **Bharosa:** medical na hona aur RMP ki zaroorat na hona: Sambhav (guidelines ka sirf saar). Muft seva CPA ke bahar: Pakka (s.2(42)). Endorsement wala hissa: Sambhav.
- **Owner ka faisla chahiye?** Haan: §5 Q1 (qualification); §4 #4 (related brand ka label).

## 5. Owner ke liye prastaavit sawal

### Q1. Skin & Hair Expert aur Beauty Advisor ki qualification

- **Sawal:** in roles ke liye koi qualification ki shart ho, aur profile pe dikhe?
- **Kyun zaroori:** "Expert" ek daawa hai; bina aadhaar ke ho to gumraah karne wala (CPA s.2(28)); aur salah ki zimmedari platform ki (§1.7).
- **Vikalp:** (a) koi shart nahi, sirf "beauty salah, medical nahi" ki line; (b) beauty ya cosmetology ka certificate zaroori, staff jaanche, profile pe dikhe (dummy); (c) (b) + saal me ek training ka record.
- **Salah:** (b).
- **Trade-off:** (a) sabse aasaan, par "Expert" shabd kamzor; (b) ek field aur jaanch ka kaam; (c) zyada record, akele owner pe bojh.

### Q2. Delivery partner ki azaadi aur exclusivity

- **Sawal:** partner kaam ki offer chhod sake (bina saza) aur doosre platforms pe bhi kaam kar sake?
- **Kyun zaroori:** contractor bane rehna (D-033) isi pe sabse zyada tika hai; control zyada to employee maane jaane ka jokhim (§1.6).
- **Vikalp:** (a) dono haan: offer chhodna aur doosre platform, dono ki azaadi; (b) offer chhodne ki azaadi, par hamare saath kaam ke ghanton me exclusivity; (c) tay shifts aur exclusivity.
- **Salah:** (a).
- **Trade-off:** (a) me din ki planning kam pakki (partner mana kar sakte); (c) planning aasaan, par employment ka jokhim aur Social Security ke alawa labour codes ka bojh.

### Q3. Partner ki gaadi aur insurance

- **Sawal:** delivery ke istemaal ka insurance kiski zimmedari ho?
- **Kyun zaroori:** private gaadi ki policy delivery ka claim mana kar sakti hai, aur kanoon ki halat Anishchit hai (§1.6).
- **Vikalp:** (a) partner ki zimmedari: licence, RC aur aisa insurance jo delivery ka istemaal cover kare, onboarding pe saboot; (b) (a) + platform ka group accident cover (asli business pe kharcha); (c) sirf cycle ya kam speed wale electric vahan.
- **Salah:** abhi (a) (dummy); asli business pe (b).
- **Trade-off:** (a) ₹0 (D-028), par partner pe bojh; (b) kharcha, par partner ki suraksha aur bharose me badhat; (c) kam jokhim, par door ke pincodes ke liye dheema.

### Q4. Seller aur partner ke saath jhagde ka aakhri raasta

- **Sawal:** platform ke andar ka dispute (D-164) na sulje, to aage kya?
- **Kyun zaroori:** seller agreement aur partner agreement me ye clause hona chahiye (§3.3 clause 24, §3.4 clause 15).
- **Vikalp:** (a) `[CITY: DUMMY]` ke court; (b) arbitration, ek arbitrator, seat `[CITY: DUMMY]`; (c) pehle mediation, phir (b).
- **Salah:** sellers ke liye (c); partners ke liye (a), kyunki arbitration ka kharcha chhote daawe pe bhaari.

### Q5. Asli business pe sellers ke paise ka alag bank account

- **Sawal:** asli business pe sellers ka paisa platform ke apne kharche wale account se alag bank account me rahe?
- **Kyun zaroori:** ledger me liability (D-256) sirf hisaab hai; kanoon me alag "trust" nahi, to platform doobe to sellers aam lenadaar (§1.3). Alag account se milaan aur bharosa saaf.
- **Vikalp:** (a) ek hi current account, ledger me alag (aaj jaisa); (b) gateway ka settlement alag "seller settlement" account me, payout usi se; (c) (b) + seller agreement me us account ke paise ko sellers ke liye rakha hua maanne ka clause.
- **Salah:** asli business pe (b); (c) lawyer ki agli jaanch ke saath.
- **Trade-off:** (a) saral; (b) ek aur khaata aur roz ka milaan; (c) sellers ki zyada suraksha, par platform pe kanooni bandish.

### Q6. Seller agreement me platform ki zimmedari ki chhat

- **Sawal:** seller ke saamne platform ki zimmedari ki koi chhat ho?
- **Kyun zaroori:** bina chhat ke ek bada daawa (jaise IP ya recall ka) platform ki kul fees se kai guna ho sakta hai; customer ke kanooni haq pe koi chhat nahi lag sakti.
- **Vikalp:** (a) koi chhat nahi; (b) pichhle 12 mahine ki us seller ki fees jitni, par D-063 ki bharpai, dhokha aur kanooni zimmedari bahar; (c) tay rakam (misaal).
- **Salah:** (b).
- **Trade-off:** (a) sellers ke liye bharosa, platform pe jokhim; (b) santulit; (c) saral par chhote ya bade sellers pe barabar nahi baithti.

## 6. CA ke liye

Ye points agle agent (CA, D-257) ke liye; Lawyer ne inpe tax ki raay nahi di.

1. **Payment collection agent aur GST:** platform sirf paisa lene ka agent (§1.3), maal ki supply ka agent nahi; isse CGST Schedule I ke "principal aur agent" wale niyam pe koi asar nahi, ye pakka karna.
2. **TCS (s.52) aur TDS (Income-tax Act 2025 s.393):** set-off (fees, udhaar, bharpai) ke baad bhi TCS aur TDS gross bikri pe; Unibelle pe bhi (D-031); return pe ulat (D-081 jod).
3. **D-063 ki bharpai:** seller ko nuksaan ki bharpai pe GST (supply hai ya nahi), aur seller ke apne ITC ulatne ka hisaab (CL-017).
4. **D-068 aur D-103:** partner ki kamaai se nuksaan ki kaat pe GST aur TDS ka asar (kaat ke baad ki rakam pe TDS ya poori pe).
5. **Delivery fee:** e-commerce operator ke zariye delivery seva ka niyam (CL-012) aur platform ki apni delivery fee ka SAC aur rate.
6. **Gift card:** co-branding ya merchant model me voucher ka GST kis pe aur kab (CL-022, Circular 243).
7. **Points:** points ki chhoot ka invoice pe ilaaj aur liability ki accounting (CL-008).
8. **MSMED Act aur income-tax s.43B(h) jaisa niyam (2025 ke Act me jo bhi ho):** sellers ke payout ya seller ki fees pe laagu hai ya nahi (§2.2 CL-126).
9. **Seller agreement aur partner agreement pe stamp duty** (Maharashtra) aur e-stamp ka record (CL-128): lawyer aur CA dono.
10. **Income-tax records ka samay** (CL-021 ka "Jaancha nahi" hissa).
11. **SPECIMEN invoices:** demo ke invoices s.122 ke bahar rehne ka saboot (CL-024).
12. **Social Security contribution:** turnover ka hisaab (tax ke bina) aur provision ka khaata (D-254).

## 7. Pehle ke kaam se lene layak (D-074)

Pehle ke kaam (purana PRD aur customer app ke purane Terms aur Privacy pages) ko sirf input ki tarah dekha. Kasauti: (1) asli zaroorat (kanoon ya tay faisla), (2) tay faislon se takrav nahi, (3) naye shabdon me likhi ja sake.

**Lene layak:**

| Cheez                                                        | (1) Zaroorat                                 | (2) Takrav nahi     | (3) Naya likhna | Kahan                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | --------------- | -------------------- |
| Seller ke bank khaate ki jaanch (penny-drop) payout se pehle | Kanoon nahi; payout ke dhokhe ki rok (D-190) | Haan (D-190 se mel) | Haan            | Seller onboarding    |
| Seller agreement ka electronic maanna (click-wrap ya e-sign) | Haan (CL-060 likhit contract, CL-128)        | Haan                | Haan            | §3.3 clause 1 aur 26 |

**Nahi lene:**

| Cheez                                                   | Kyun nahi                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Purane Terms aur Privacy pages ka text                  | Bahut patla (paanch chhote hisse); naye dhaanche (§3.1, §3.2) se pura nahi; copy ka sawal hi nahi. |
| "Legal & Compliance Officer" role aur "external lawyer" | Kanoon role ka naam nahi maangta; D-257 (AI Lawyer) aur D-237 (Grievance Officer, nodal person).   |
| Data Protection Officer ka role                         | Sirf Significant Data Fiduciary pe zaroori (CL-103); sampark Grievance Officer (D-253).            |
| Seller ka Udyam number zaroori                          | 2027 ka niyam "GSTIN ya MSME number jaisa sarkari number" kehta hai (CL-060), sirf Udyam nahi.     |
