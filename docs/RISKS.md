## 2. Jaanch (2026-11-17)

### 2.1 MongoDB Atlas free cluster

- Ek project me ek free cluster; har free cluster 0.5 GB storage, 100 operations/second, aur rolling 7 din me 10 GB andar + 10 GB bahar data transfer. Seema paar hone pe Atlas network dheema karta hai. Source: [Atlas free cluster limitations](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/free-shared-limitations/).
- Ek organization me kitne projects ban sakte hain, ye nahi jaancha.

### 2.2 Render free web service aur Key Value

- 15 minute bina traffic ke service so jaati hai; uthne me ~1 minute. Workspace ko mahine ke 750 free instance hours; soi service ghante nahi khaati; ghante khatam to saari free web services agle mahine tak band. Source: [Render: Deploy for Free](https://render.com/docs/free).
- Free Key Value: ek workspace me ek hi; disk pe save nahi, restart pe saara data jaata hai; Render kabhi bhi restart kar sakta hai. Source: [Render: Deploy for Free](https://render.com/docs/free).
- Key Value bhi 750 ghante khata hai ya nahi: sources me saaf nahi; Render dashboard pe dekhna hai.
- Keep-alive ping pe Render ka koi likhit niyam (mana ya ijazat) nahi mila.

### 2.3 Free PostgreSQL

- Render ka free PostgreSQL banne ke 30 din baad expire; 14 din ki grace, phir data samet delete. Source: [Render changelog](https://render.com/changelog/free-postgresql-instances-now-expire-after-30-days-previously-90).
- Neon ~0.5 GB, idle pe compute so jaata; Supabase 500 MB per project (2 projects), 7 din bina kaam ke pause; Aiven 1 GB se 5 GB (sources me farak), inactive pe pause. Ye sab teesre-paksh ke comparison se hain, providers ke apne pages se nahi: [Koyeb: free PostgreSQL tiers 2026](https://www.koyeb.com/blog/top-postgresql-database-free-tiers-in-2026), [Bytebase: Postgres hosting comparison](https://www.bytebase.com/blog/postgres-hosting-options-pricing-comparison/).
