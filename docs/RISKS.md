## 2. Jaanch (2026-11-17)

### 2.1 MongoDB Atlas free cluster

- Ek project me ek free cluster; har free cluster 0.5 GB storage, 100 operations/second, aur rolling 7 din me 10 GB andar + 10 GB bahar data transfer. Seema paar hone pe Atlas network dheema karta hai. Source: [Atlas free cluster limitations](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/free-shared-limitations/).
- Ek organization me 250 projects tak (badhai ja sakti hai), aur ek project me 25 clusters tak. D-011 ki MongoDB wali 8 services = 8 projects, is seema ke andar. Poore organization pe free clusters ki koi alag ginti ki seema nahi mili. Source: [Atlas Service Limits](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/atlas-limits/) (jaancha 2026-11-18).

### 2.2 Render free web service aur Key Value

- 15 minute bina traffic ke service so jaati hai; uthne me ~1 minute. Workspace ko mahine ke 750 free instance hours; soi service ghante nahi khaati; ghante khatam to saari free web services agle mahine tak band. Source: [Render: Deploy for Free](https://render.com/docs/free).
- Free Key Value: ek workspace me ek hi; disk pe save nahi, restart pe saara data jaata hai; Render kabhi bhi restart kar sakta hai. Source: [Render: Deploy for Free](https://render.com/docs/free).
- Key Value bhi 750 ghante khata hai ya nahi (jaancha 2026-11-18): Render ke docs ghanton ko sirf free web services se jodte hain; Key Value aur Postgres ke hisse me ghanton ka zikr nahi. Par docs saaf mana bhi nahi karte. Owner ne maana: isi samajh pe aage badho; Render dashboard ke Billing page pe ek baar dekh lena. Source: [Render: Deploy for Free](https://render.com/docs/free).
- Keep-alive ping pe Render ka koi likhit niyam (mana ya ijazat) nahi mila.

### 2.3 Free PostgreSQL (jaancha 2026-11-18, providers ke apne docs se)

- **Render:** free PostgreSQL banne ke 30 din baad expire; 14 din ki grace, phir data samet delete. Source: [Render changelog](https://render.com/changelog/free-postgresql-instances-now-expire-after-30-days-previously-90).
- **Neon (chuna, D-057):** free plan expire nahi hota; kaam na ho to project na pause na delete. 100 projects, har project 1 GB storage (Neon ke ek FAQ me 0.5 GB likha; docs 1 GB kehte hain) aur mahine ke 100 CU-ghante. 1 CU ≈ 4 GB RAM; sabse chhota 0.25 CU; free me load pe 2 CU tak. 5 minute bina query ke compute so jaata hai (free me band nahi hota), agli query pe kuch sau milliseconds me jaagta hai. Ghante khatam hone pe compute agle mahine tak band; data delete nahi. Sources: [Neon plans](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/plans), [Neon free plan limits](https://neon.com/faqs/free-plan-limits-and-quotas), [Neon pricing](https://neon.com/pricing), [Neon usage calculations](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/usage-calculations).
- **Supabase:** 500 MB per project, 2 free projects; 7 din bina kaam ke project pause, 1 saal tak haath se restore. Sources: [Supabase project pausing](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing), [Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing).
- **Aiven:** 1 GB storage, 1 CPU; idle pe service band, haath se chalu. Source: [Aiven PostgreSQL free tier](https://aiven.io/docs/products/postgresql/concepts/pg-free-tier).

### 2.4 Regions (jaancha 2026-11-18)

- **Render:** Oregon, Ohio, Virginia, Frankfurt aur Singapore; India nahi. Service ka region baad me nahi badalta. Source: [Render regions](https://render.com/docs/regions).
- **Atlas:** MongoDB ki AWS regions ki table me Singapore (`ap-southeast-1`) aur Mumbai (`ap-south-1`) dono hain, par free cluster wala column saaf nahi dikha. Owner AWS Singapore me free cluster pehle bana chuke hain, aur 2026-11-18 ko owner ne dobara dekha ki free cluster Singapore me available hai. Mumbai ki zaroorat nahi, kyunki database server (Singapore) ke paas hona chahiye. Source: [Atlas: AWS regions](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/amazon-aws/).
- **Neon:** AWS Singapore (`aws-ap-southeast-1`) hai; project ka region banate waqt tay hota hai aur baad me nahi badalta. Source: [Neon regions](https://neon.com/docs/introduction/regions).
